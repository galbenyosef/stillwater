# Sound recordings

These local loops adapt CC0 1.0 field recordings already used in Stillwater's film work. They were trimmed, filtered, level-matched and crossfaded for looping.

- **birds.ogg** — [lakeshore-birds](https://freesound.org/people/pwerrell/sounds/368807/) by pwerrell; source excerpt 10–32s. Source SHA-256: `ba7ea394d8436f2c9829138ed53dcf1eebacf8091c0db6c48af8d235347f35cd`.
- **water.ogg** — [wheel-waterfall](https://freesound.org/people/Tom_Kaszuba/sounds/660255/) by Tom_Kaszuba; source excerpt 3–27s. Source SHA-256: `cb2acc1348c4658e7c0d8c7e9e4ee9e0b0bfd76260fe7c57b16b3ed09ce25262`.
- **motor.ogg** — [shore-motor](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/637743/) by kyles; source excerpt 23–35s. Source SHA-256: `a274534e14ec010262592ebf271e6e04c08c85659c6c59838f09d9d6b1a55589`.

All three sources: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The mill mechanism's stone rumble, axle friction, gear contacts and timber creaks
are original procedural synthesis in `src/mill-audio.mjs`, covered by the app's
MIT license. They contain no sampled recordings or third-party sound assets.
